Five Reflections

Five works from Reflection by Pindar Van Arman were acquired for the permanent collection of the Museum of Crypto Art. Reflection is a fully on-chain execution of Van Arman’s reflective AI process.

    Pindar Van Arman, Reflection, 2024. Acquired by the Museum of Crypto Art. Each work is generated on mint from the transaction hash and a set of physical inputs from the robotic system, composed in Solidity and stored on Ethereum with no external dependencies.
