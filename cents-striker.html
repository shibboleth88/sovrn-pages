How the strike works

Blank

Eight cents from the CENTS corpus, photographed, with Lincoln lifted off the field. The rim, the legends and the date are untouched — they are still each coin's own.

Strike

Your avatar becomes a height map. Surface normals are lit from wherever your cursor is, then multiplied into the copper underneath.

Patina

Because the shading modulates real pixels rather than replacing them, the coin's toning and scratches carry straight through your portrait.

Nothing is uploaded. The die lives in this page — your image is read by the browser, struck on your own machine, and forgotten when you close the tab.
