Where the work comes from

These three collections keep their artwork inside the contract. Asking one of them for a work returns the image itself — a vector drawing, encoded into the reply — rather than an address to go and fetch it from somewhere else. Nothing sits on a server, and nothing can be moved or taken down. It is also what makes these shareable at all: the picture arrives as data, so the browser is free to redraw it as a GIF. Sovrn's other collections keep their images elsewhere, which is why they are not here.
