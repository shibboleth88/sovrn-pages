POLY. A Fluid Show

17 September 2023 – 25 February 2024

Mikey Woodbridge showed the full collection Latent Couture in POLY. A Fluid Show at the KINDL in Berlin, presented as a wall-scale printed grid of the AI-generated looks, and performed at both the opening and the closing of the exhibition. Curated by Solvej Helweg Ovesen, the show gathered eleven international artists working transdisciplinarily outward from painting, proposing a polycentric culture set against monoculture — monogamy, minimalism, specialisation. Its subject was hustling, mixing and queering: the dissolving of boundaries between countries, genders, generations, materials, and between digital and analogue cultures.
