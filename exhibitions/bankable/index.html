The year on the coin

Basel Social Club took its 2025 theme from the language of finance and exchange — systems of value, commerce and trade — and set it inside an actual disused bank. BANKABLE answered with a room of works about money: Damien Hirst’s The Currency, a slot machine that pays out from real wallets, transaction lines drawn between addresses.

The CENTS hung there were struck in 1971 — the year the United States left the gold standard and money became a purely abstract construct. In a show about what makes a thing bankable, the coins on the wall were the ones minted at the moment value came loose from metal.

Basel Social Club

In its fourth edition Basel Social Club inhabited the former private bank Vontobel in the centre of Grossbasel, spanning more than a hundred rooms and turning the building into a tableau vivant. It was the first to open those premises to the public, marking the start of the building’s activation by FOR ART, Klaus Littmann’s project to revive the vacant property on Rittergasse through to the end of 2028.

Rooms ran from a working blood bank to spaces for jewellery, beauty, wellness and games, with durational performances throughout. Admission was free and open to all.
