Vitra Design Museum · Vitra Schaudepot, Weil am Rhein

Science Fiction Design: From Space Age to Metaverse

18 May 2024 – 10 May 2026

Twenty-one works from Latent Couture by Mikey Woodbridge were exhibited in Science Fiction Design: From Space Age to Metaverse at the Vitra Schaudepot for two years from May 2024 through May 2026. The exhibition set over 100 objects from the museum’s collection within a futuristic display, tracing the exchange between science fiction and design from the early twentieth century through the Space Age to work made for virtual worlds. Latent Couture appeared among the contemporary futurisms closing the show, alongside Andrés Reisinger’s The Shipping and Hortensia Chair, ZYVA Studio and Charlotte Taylor’s Neo-Chemosphere, and Francis Bitonti’s Molecule Shoes.

In the coverage

Reisinger described the display as evoking “multiple realities and timelines intertwining” within the present. Writing for the Deutscher Designer Club, Thomas Wagner noted that the show turns, briefly but precisely, toward present-day futurisms — NFT-based art in digital space and Afrofuturism, traced from Sun Ra’s Space Is the Place (1974) onward. That is the register Latent Couture was shown in: not the Space Age lineage of Aarnio, Colombo and Panton that fills most of the exhibition, but the closing argument about who imagines the future now.
