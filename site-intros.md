# Native Sites intros for the collection pages

Short prose to paste into each Google Sites page as a **normal text block** —
not an embed. The point is that sovrn.art owns some indexable words of its own:
the collection pages currently put all their text inside an iframe served from
shibboleth88.github.io, where Google is likely to attribute it to that domain
instead.

**Where to put it:** below the embed. The embed carries its own hero and title,
so text above it would push the design down the page.

**Why no heading:** a visible `Collection name` heading would duplicate the title
already inside the embed. Each paragraph instead opens by naming the artist, the
collection and the date in ordinary prose, which puts the searchable words on the
page without repeating anything on screen.

Emerging Faces is not listed — it lives on vanarman.com, so there is no sovrn.art
page to add text to.

## CENTS — Rutherford Chang
`https://www.sovrn.art/cents`

CENTS is a work by Rutherford Chang: 10,000 copper pennies removed from
circulation, documented one by one, and inscribed as ordinals on 10,000 satoshis,
the smallest units of Bitcoin. The coins themselves were smelted into a 69-pound
copper block, inscribed in turn as the entirety of Bitcoin block #839969 and
offered at Christie's in June 2024. The performance of value.

---

## Reflection — Pindar Van Arman
`https://www.sovrn.art/curated/reflection`

Reflection is Pindar Van Arman's Reflective AI process, executed fully on-chain,
released on Ethereum in February 2024. Each work is generated from data held in
the contract itself rather than stored elsewhere — a continuation of the question
Van Arman has pursued since he began working with painting robots in 2006: how a
generative process can transcend algorithmic determinism and reach pure emergence.

---

## Wunderkammer — Isa Kost
`https://www.sovrn.art/curated/wunderkammer`

Wunderkammer is an on-chain Cabinet of Wonders by Isa Kost: 108 objects released
on Ethereum in January 2025, each a dead memory that has called out to be found.
Kost's practice is a self-archeology defined by a deep connection with death,
where finding speaks to what is lost. Sacred Foot, from the collection, is held in
the permanent collection of the Francisco Carolinum, Linz.

---

## Painting with Fire: a History in GANs — Bård Ionson
`https://www.sovrn.art/curated/painting-with-fire`

Painting with Fire: a History in GANs is a collection of 200 works by Bård Ionson,
released on Ethereum in December 2023. It traces the evolution of generative
adversarial networks from 2014 to 2023 through fourteen vintage GAN models, each
invoking the same elemental image of fire, and every work carries a Historic Event
trait tying it to a moment in that timeline.

---

## Noctilucent Mementi — Martin Lukas Ostachowski
`https://www.sovrn.art/curated/mementi`

Noctilucent Mementi is a generative chronicle of five years of travel and work
with clouds by Martin Lukas Ostachowski, released on Ethereum in October 2023. In
the artist's words, noctilucent clouds are invisible memories, glowing when
revisited.

---

## Possibility Spaces — Look Highward
`https://www.sovrn.art/curated/possibility-spaces`

Possibility Spaces is a collection of 555 large-scale works by Look Highward,
released on Ethereum in August 2023, many of them surpassing 250 million pixels.
The worlds were evolved through an emergent series of calls and responses between
artist and AI — spiritual experience seen through the mind's eye of machine
learning.

---

## Sightseers: Perimeter Town — Norman Harman
`https://www.sovrn.art/curated/perimeter-town`

Sightseers: Perimeter Town is a boutique set by Norman Harman, released on
Ethereum in June 2023, drawn from the perimeter of the SIGHTSEERS world. Harman
strays out further from the motel to pluck the outgrowths of the glitched and
morphic wastelands: the refined emergences at the edge of that world.

---

## Seasons of Mobility — bashobits
`https://www.sovrn.art/curated/seasons-of-mobility`

Seasons of Mobility is a collection by bashobits, released on Ethereum in April
2023. It is the year 1010 After Humanity — we are long gone, but the autonomous
trains, buses and cars we created have become a new species that calls itself
Mobility. It was made in the aesthetic tradition of wabi-sabi, which finds beauty
in the imperfect, the impermanent and the incomplete; Admiral Rush is held in the
permanent collection of the Francisco Carolinum, Linz.

---

## Latent Couture — Mikey Woodbridge
`https://www.sovrn.art/curated/latent-couture`

Latent Couture is a collection of 555 AI fashion statements by Mikey Woodbridge,
released on Ethereum in March 2023 and generated from photographs of Woodbridge's
years as a performer, painter, designer and nightlife icon. The opposite of a
deepfake, it reaches into the latent space to find the deepreal — each piece says,
"This person is possible." The full collection was shown at KINDL — Zentrum für
zeitgenössische Kunst, Berlin, in POLY. A Fluid Show, September 2023 to February
2024.

---

## byteGANs — Pindar Van Arman
`https://www.sovrn.art/curated/bytegans`

byteGANs is the first fully on-chain AI art collection on Ethereum, by Pindar Van
Arman, released in February 2023. Each work is a kilobyte on the ledger — an
eleven-pixel-square animation of eleven frames — dancing forever dances.

---

## RABBIT TAKEOVER — Anne Spalter
`https://www.sovrn.art/curated/rabbit-takeover`

RABBIT TAKEOVER is a collection by Anne Spalter on Ethereum, following a lone
rabbit cavorting about a post-armageddon world, travelling through swirling
trans-dimensional portals to preside over scenes of mayhem and destruction. The
rabbit is based on Spalter's pet, Pickles.

---

## SIGHTSEERS — Norman Harman
`https://www.sovrn.art/curated/sightseers`

SIGHTSEERS is Norman Harman's defining work from the early wave of AI cryptoart,
released on Ethereum in October 2022. Harman mixes painting, glitch art and AI to
build a world based on the sightseers who came to watch the nuclear bomb tests
outside Las Vegas. Works from the collection are held in the permanent collections
of Arab Bank Switzerland and the Francisco Carolinum, Linz.

---

## cope. Vol 1 — aleqth
`https://www.sovrn.art/curated/cope-vol-1`

cope. Vol 1 is a book made and self-published by aleqth in October 2021, its pages
created day by day and the making live-shared page by page. Each page, and both
covers, is tokenized on the blockchain; the collection was released on Ethereum in
September 2022.

## AI Spaceships — Anne Spalter
`https://www.sovrn.art/curated/ai-spaceships`

AI Spaceships is a collection by Anne Spalter, released on Ethereum in June 2022.
Climate change has made Earth uninhabitable and the remaining people build ships
to leave: some fail at launch and go down in flames, others warp to steampunk
battleships, or to neon hyperspace.
