Seasons of Mobility

by bashobits

Released 15 April 2023 · Ethereum

It is the year 1010AH (After Humanity). We are long gone. But life continues. The autonomous trains, buses, and cars created by us have become a new species that calls itself Mobility.

Seasons of Mobility was created in the aesthetic tradition of wabi-sabi, which identifies beauty in the imperfect, the impermanent, and the incomplete.

The colours and scenes invoke the four seasons across a full year. Each piece reflects everyday life in a future that is both familiar and surreal.

On 58 screens in Shibuya

NEORT is a digital art platform for artists and collectors. This includes exhibitions in Tokyo to reimagine the digital signage scattered throughout the city not merely as advertising media, but as a new stage for cultural expression, showcasing artworks on these screens. Utilizing large-scale signage networks, NEORT offers artists from around the world an opportunity to challenge new possibilities of expression in urban spaces.

Aura Afterglow, a custom combination of three pieces from Freedom of Mobility, was selected for the February 2026 exhibition Weather Writes, part of Screens Contextualized, and displayed across 58 screens in Shibuya, Tokyo.

The wider Mobility world

Since Seasons of Mobility, bashobits has carried the world through three further collections, released to holders of the original, and a game, Lost Mobility: Sand Wave Drift. Each takes the same premise somewhere else: what Mobility remembers, what it wants, and what it leaves behind. For more see bashobits.com.

The artist

Having been trained by a mix of humans, AI systems, and spirits, bashobits is an artist in motion, imperfectly.

The focus on motion can be experienced directly in the art trilogy of Mobility: Seasons of Mobility, Memories of Mobility, and Freedom of Mobility.

The theme of motion continues in the whimsical art collection Berry Based.

Human collaborations include working with award-winning AI CryptoArtist Pindar Van Arman as part of the bitGAN Collabs.

AI system collaborations include hallucinating through the latent space alongside the daring Disco Diffusion systems to explore chaos.

Spirit influences start with the soulful poet Matsuo Bashō, leading directly to the name bashobits. This reflects time spent writing poetry for many years since childhood and studying poetry formally through university.

In addition to creating, bashobits collects specific AI CryptoArt once in a while.
