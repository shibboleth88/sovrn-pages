Mobility

by bashobits

Four collections · 2023–2025 · Ethereum

It is the year 1010AH (After Humanity). We are long gone. But life continues. The autonomous trains, buses, and cars created by us have become a new species that calls itself Mobility.

Mobility holds that life is motion. It moves through a world it inherited from us, carries memories of a past it may be misremembering, and wants the freedom to go where it chooses. Each collection takes up one of those.

Behind them is bashobits, who works at the intersection of movement and mindfulness and takes the name from Matsuo Bashō, whose wanderlust the work inherits. The world has grown across four collections since 2023, in mixed media and generative systems, all in the aesthetic tradition of wabi-sabi — beauty in the imperfect, the impermanent and the incomplete.

The collections

Four so far, each taking the same premise somewhere else: what Mobility lives through, what it remembers, what it wants, and what it leaves behind. For more see bashobits.com.

What Mobility remembers

Memories of Mobility · 105 works · 2023

The memories reach back more than two thousand years before 1010AH, and Mobility does not trust them. They carry the faults memory has in us: they may be mistaken, or imagined rather than recalled.

What is striking is what survived. Fifteen of the 105 titles are the vocabulary of our digital routine — not our wars or our cities, but our admin.

  • Configuring Emails
  • Decoding Comments
  • Bookmarks of Change
  • Scrolling to Acquire Signals
  • Disarranging Retweets, Deciding Futures
  • Exploiting Unfollowed Posts
  • Safeguard Adaptive Hashtags
  • Enlightenment in Discovered Annotations
  • Repurposing Summaries, Uploading
  • Ignoring the Tag

A vocabulary that recombines

Freedom of Mobility · 365 works · 2024

Mobility holds that life is motion, and wants the freedom of it. The question is put to us as much as to them: whether a digital creation should live where its maker decides, or where a company does.

The 365 titles are built from 183 words, turned over and recombined — the same terms recurring in new company, which is the shape a species with freedom of movement would leave behind.

On 58 screens in Shibuya

NEORT is a digital art platform for artists and collectors. This includes exhibitions in Tokyo to reimagine the digital signage scattered throughout the city not merely as advertising media, but as a new stage for cultural expression, showcasing artworks on these screens. Utilizing large-scale signage networks, NEORT offers artists from around the world an opportunity to challenge new possibilities of expression in urban spaces.

Aura Afterglow, a custom combination of three pieces from Freedom of Mobility, was selected for the February 2026 exhibition Weather Writes, part of Screens Contextualized, and displayed across 58 screens in Shibuya, Tokyo.

How a Ghost is named

Ghosts of Mobility · 366 works · 2025 · fully on-chain

Nine backdrops, nine spacetimes, five locations, three entropies and five skies — 6,075 sentences the grammar can make, of which 366 were made.

Entropy is not a mood but a path. Balance is a sine wave, and it is the one the traffic crossing this page is travelling on.

The artist

Having been trained by a mix of humans, AI systems, and spirits, bashobits is an artist in motion, imperfectly.

The focus on motion can be experienced directly in the art trilogy of Mobility: Seasons of Mobility, Memories of Mobility, and Freedom of Mobility.

The theme of motion continues in the whimsical art collection Berry Based.

Human collaborations include working with award-winning AI CryptoArtist Pindar Van Arman as part of the bitGAN Collabs.

AI system collaborations include hallucinating through the latent space alongside the daring Disco Diffusion systems to explore chaos.

Spirit influences start with the soulful poet Matsuo Bashō, leading directly to the name bashobits. This reflects time spent writing poetry for many years since childhood and studying poetry formally through university.

In addition to creating, bashobits collects specific AI CryptoArt once in a while.
