CENTS Block Copper & CENTS Block 839969

Rutherford Chang · the object and its inscription

The object

CENTS Block Copper

The 10,000 copper Lincoln cents that comprise the digital set CENTS were destroyed and smelted into a 68.56-pound block, titled CENTS Block Copper. This object resides with the Estate of Rutherford Chang.

The inscription

CENTS Block 839969

The physical block was rendered as a three-dimensional digital model and in turn inscribed as a 4-megabyte ordinal, comprising the entirety of Bitcoin block #839969. This object was auctioned at Christie’s Beyond the Screen and resides in a private collection.
